Term Insurance Kya Hota Hai?

Term insurance ek life insurance product hai jisme policy terms ke according death benefit ka provision hota hai.

Policy samajhne ke points

Sum assured, policy term, premium, exclusions, nominee details aur policy wording ko samjhein. Premium aur eligibility insurer ki underwriting aur applicable terms par depend karte hain.

Important

Policy lene se pehle insurer ke official policy documents aur applicable disclosures ko carefully read karein. Guaranteed claim ya approval assume na karein.
