SIP Kya Hai?

SIP ka full form Systematic Investment Plan hai. Iske through mutual fund mein fixed amount ko periodic basis par invest kiya ja sakta hai.

SIP samajhne ke points

Investment amount, frequency, selected mutual fund scheme, risk level, charges aur exit-related conditions ko samjhein. Mutual fund returns market-linked hote hain.

Risk

Past performance future returns ki guarantee nahi hoti. Investment se pehle scheme documents aur risk factors padhein.
