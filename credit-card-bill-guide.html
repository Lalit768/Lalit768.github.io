Credit Card Bill Kaise Samjhein?

Credit card statement mein total amount due, minimum amount due, payment due date aur transactions jaise details hoti hain.

Statement mein kya dekhein

Payment due date aur total amount due ko dhyan se check karein. Minimum amount due sirf minimum payment requirement ho sakta hai; interest aur charges se bachne ke liye card issuer ke terms samjhein.

Safety

OTP, PIN, CVV, password aur full card number kisi ke saath share na karein.
