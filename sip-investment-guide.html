SIP Investment Kaise Kaam Karta Hai?

AMFI ke mutabik SIP ek methodology hai jisme mutual fund scheme mein fixed amount ko fixed intervals par invest kiya ja sakta hai.

SIP mein kya samjhein?

  • Investment amount aur frequency
  • Selected mutual fund scheme
  • Risk level aur investment objective
  • Applicable charges aur scheme documents

Important

Mutual fund investments market-linked hote hain. Past performance future returns ki guarantee nahi hoti. Investment se pehle scheme ke risk factors aur documents padhein.
