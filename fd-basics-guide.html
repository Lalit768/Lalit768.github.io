FD kya hoti hai?

Fixed Deposit mein ek amount ko specified tenure ke liye bank ya financial institution ke applicable terms par deposit kiya ja sakta hai.

Basic points

Tenure, interest rate, payout option, premature withdrawal conditions aur applicable charges ko deposit se pehle samjhein.

Interest rate aur FD terms institution ke current rules aur product conditions par depend karte hain.
