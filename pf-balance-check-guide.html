PF Balance Kaise Check Karein?

EPF member apne PF balance aur passbook se contribution details dekh sakta hai. EPFO ka official Member Passbook portal available hai.

Basic process

  1. Official EPFO Member Passbook portal kholen.
  2. UAN aur password se sign in karein.
  3. Passbook mein available contribution details check karein.

Safety

UAN password, Aadhaar, PAN, bank details ya OTP kisi unknown person ke saath share na karein. EPFO members ko credential fraud se alert rehne ko kehta hai.

Official source: EPFO Member Passbook
